In progress

Continuous Cognitive Heterogeneity in Dementia: A Severity Factor and an Age-Related Amnestic Axis Without Reproducible Subtypes

Oct 2024–present

Hanlong Chen∗,†, Ian Zhang∗, …, Malcolm Binns

Rotman Research Institute, Baycrest · Supervisor: Dr. Malcolm Binns

Whether the cognitive profiles of people living with dementia form reproducible discrete subtypes. Under cross-validated joint selection of PCA dimensionality and cluster number, the data point instead to a continuous severity factor and an age-related amnestic axis, with no subtype solution that replicates.

Apr 2025–present

Hanlong Chen, Bruce Liu, Aya Mitani, Karim Keshavjee, Felipe Cepeda, Aziz Guergachi, Camellia Zakaria

Canadian Primary Care Sentinel Surveillance Network (CPCSSN) · Supervisors: Dr. Camellia Zakaria and Dr. Aya Mitani
